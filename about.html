About Threadline

Good clothes shouldn't be complicated.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make simple, well-made clothing that fits real life. We focus on timeless basics that are comfortable to wear every day and priced so that anyone can build a wardrobe they love.

Our Story

Threadline started with a simple frustration: finding a good white tee or a pair of jeans that lasted shouldn't be this hard. We began as a small shop in Charlotte, NC, picking only the pieces we would wear ourselves, and we have been growing one hoodie at a time ever since.

What We Value

  • Quality materials that hold up wash after wash
  • Versatile styles that mix and match
  • Fair, transparent prices
